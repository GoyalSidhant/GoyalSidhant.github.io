Founding GrowthOS started with one simple question: **what if growth teams could run repeatable, high-quality workflows with autonomous support rather than one-off manual hustle?**

We are building toward three principles:

1. **Operator-first design**: every automation should feel like an extension of a skilled GTM operator.
2. **Fast iteration loops**: product improvements should ship in days, not quarters.
3. **Reliable infrastructure**: growth systems only matter if they run consistently under scale.

The interesting part is that this is not just an AI problem. It is an architecture problem, a data quality problem, and an execution discipline problem.

As we continue building, my focus remains the same: combine founder velocity with engineering rigor so each feature moves the business forward.
